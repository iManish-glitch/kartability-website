01 / THE IDEA

Fun should have
more than one way in.

KartAbility is a concept for an indoor adaptive go-kart program where people with and without disabilities can enjoy recreation side by side.

We want the experience to feel welcoming from the moment someone arrives: a clear route in, support when needed, and a chance to drive in a way that works for them.
